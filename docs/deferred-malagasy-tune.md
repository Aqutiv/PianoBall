# Deferred Malagasy candidate

**Not cleared or implemented:** “Iny hono izy ravorombazaha” (also written “Iny Hono Izy Ravorombazaha”). No playable ID, chart, audio asset or exported entry is included.

This traditional Malagasy lullaby is a promising familiar choice. A [Malagasy recording publisher describes it as the country's most famous lullaby](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=pprTcOzJCMw); that supports broad recognition, not a claim that literally everyone knows it. A [public-domain sheet-music catalog offers the melody](https://www.partitions-domaine-public.fr/pdf/9588/madagascar-iny-hono-izy-ravorombazaha.html), but that label alone does not establish unrestricted use within Madagascar.

The unresolved issue is Madagascar's treatment of folklore. [WIPO's Madagascar law entry](https://www.wipo.int/en/web/traditional-knowledge/w/tklaws/article_0053) includes Article 5's protection of expressions of folklore. The [OMDA statute, Article 3(h)](https://wipolex-res.wipo.int/edocs/lexdocs/laws/fr/mg/mg015fr.pdf), also discusses administration of folklore and a paying public domain. These provisions require local clarification before treating this melody as cleared for the proposed distribution.

Per the approved plan, defer inclusion until the relevant folklore authorization/payment requirements are established. Do not infer clearance from a tune's traditional origin or a foreign sheet-music website's public-domain label. No OMDA contact or licensing request has been made on the user's behalf.
